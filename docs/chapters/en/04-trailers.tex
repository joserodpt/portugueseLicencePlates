\section{Trailers}

\subsection{Civil trailers}

Civil trailer plates follow their own sequences: \plate{X-0} to \plate{X-000000},
or \plate{XX-0} to \plate{XX-00000}, where \plate{X} or \plate{XX} is the code of
the district or regional office where the trailer was registered (see
appendix~\ref{sec:distritos}).

\subsection{Military trailers}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Army trailers have the sequences \plate{0-M}, \plate{00-M}, \plate{000-M}
        and \plate{0000-M}.
  \item Air Force trailers have the sequence \plate{E-00-00}.
  \item Navy trailers share the sequence of the Navy's cars: \plate{AP-00-00}.
\end{itemize}

The number is shown on a plate of the same format and colours as a car's, but
without the yellow band with the registration date. Because the rule is not
widely known, trailer plates with the yellow band and date are common.
