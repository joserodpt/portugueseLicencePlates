\section{Legislation}\label{sec:legislacao}

\subsection{Law in force}

\textbf{Decree-Law 54/2005 of 3 March}\footnote{\url{https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2005/03/044a00/18921897.pdf}}
approves the \emph{Regulation on the Registration Number and Plate of Motor Cars,
their Trailers, and Motorcycles, Tricycles and Quadricycles over 50~cm³}. The main
articles of the Regulation are:

\begin{description}
  \item[Art. 3, registration number.] Two groups of two digits and one group of
    two letters, separated by dashes. Where the letters go depends on the date:
    \plate{AA-00-00} until 29/02/1992, \plate{00-00-AA} from 01/03/1992, and
    \plate{00-AA-00} once that series ran out. Paragraph~3 already provided that,
    once \plate{00-AA-00} ran out, the number would become two groups of two
    letters around a central group of two digits (\plate{AA-00-AA}).
  \item[Art. 4, trailers and export.] Trailers have one or two letters of the
    regional office (annex~I) followed by a serial number; vehicles for export, a
    serial number followed by the initial of Lisbon, Porto, Azores or Madeira.
  \item[Art. 5, the plate.] The model depends on the registration date: black
    background with white characters until 31/12/1991 (annex~II); retroreflective,
    white background with black characters and border, from 01/01/1992 to
    31/12/1997 (annex~III); the same, with the year and month of first
    registration on the right-hand end, from 01/01/1998 (annex~IV). Older plates
    may be replaced by later models.
  \item[Art. 6, special cases.] Export: yellow plate with black characters
    (annex~V). Diplomatic and consular corps, foreign mission staff and bodies
    covered by the Protocol on the Privileges and Immunities of the European
    Communities: red characters, dashes and border.
  \item[Art. 7, fitting.] Cars carry two plates, one at the front and one at the
    back.
\end{description}

The decree-law itself repeals articles 1 to 3 of Regulatory Decree 13/98 and
articles 35 and 37 of the 1954 Highway Code Regulation (art.~5), and sets
01/01/2006 as the start date for the rules on selling plates (art.~6).

\subsection{Amendments}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.3\linewidth} p{0.64\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Law} & \textbf{What changed} \\
\midrule\endhead
\bottomrule\endfoot
DL 106/2006 of 8 June & Extended the regulation to mopeds and revised motorcycle plates. \\
DL 112/2009 of 18 May & 2nd amendment; kept the 1991 and 1997 dates in art.~5. \\
Law 46/2010 of 7 September & 3rd amendment. \\
DL 2/2020 of 14 January & 4th amendment. Brought the \plate{AA 00 AA} format into use, removed the dashes and the month-and-year band, and brought the plate closer to the model used by most EU member states. Repealed paragraphs~5 and~7 of art.~5 of the Regulation. \\
DL 9/2021 & Listed in the consolidated version of 29/01/2021; what it changed for plates is unconfirmed. \\
\end{longtable}

According to IMT, the \plate{AA 00 AA} format applies only to new vehicles; the
owner of a vehicle from an earlier series can ask for a plate without the month
and year. We could not confirm whether there have been amendments since 2021.
